# Tea-field artwork

`tea-landscape.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for the
reference-based AgriPulse redesign. It is decorative artwork, not a photograph of
any user's registered farm. Used in the sidebar, welcome banner, and farm-list
thumbnails. No application statistics or interface text are baked into the image.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: decorative scenic landscape photo for an agricultural dashboard sidebar and a narrow welcome banner. A lush Sri Lankan tea plantation with gracefully curved rows of vivid green tea bushes in the foreground, a large shade tree at middle left, rolling green hills and softly misty mountains in the distance, pale warm morning sky. Premium natural editorial landscape photography, soft morning sunlight, muted forest and sage greens, realistic textures, serene hopeful atmosphere. Wide landscape composition, no people, no buildings, no text, no logos, no watermark. This is a background image asset only, not a UI mockup.
